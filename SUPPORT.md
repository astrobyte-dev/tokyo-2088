# TOKYO 2088 support

Contact Astrobyte at [thr3eoh@gmail.com](mailto:thr3eoh@gmail.com).

## Settings

Open TOKYO 2088 Settings in the Connect IQ Store app, choose your options, then save and sync. Select Custom or Manual city to use the corresponding text fields.

The clock follows your watch's local time. The Tokyo theme and manual city label do not change the time zone.

## Trial and unlocking

TOKYO 2088 has a 24-hour trial, then a one-time US$2.50 unlock through KiezelPay. There is no subscription. Check the final checkout total for applicable taxes or currency conversion.

After the trial, enter the code shown on your watch at [kzl.io/code](https://kzl.io/code). Keep the watch connected through Garmin Connect for activation. Allow around five minutes for the next check; network delays can take longer.

For checkout or purchase recovery, see [KiezelPay's help page](https://kiezelpay.com/faq/). If you have already purchased and see an unexpected payment prompt, use purchase recovery or contact support before paying again.

## Reporting a problem

Include your watch model, watch-face version and a short description of what happened. A photo of the display can help; hide any personal text first.

Do not send payment credentials, licence tokens, raw health/location logs or other sensitive information. Email support rather than posting personal details publicly.

See the [privacy notice](PRIVACY.md) for information about display data, licensing and support messages.
